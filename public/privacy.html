Privacy Policy

BeerReel ("we", "our", "the app") is a social app for checking in, rating, and sharing beers with friends. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. BeerReel is intended for people of legal drinking age only.

Information we collect

  • Account info: username, password, and optionally your real name, email address, bio, and profile photo.
  • Content you post: beer check-ins (photos, ratings, comments), reactions, and comments on other people's posts.
  • Social graph: your friend connections, friend requests, and any accounts you've blocked.
  • Reports: if you report a post or a user, we store the reason and any details you provide so it can be reviewed.
  • Device/notification info: a push notification token, if you enable notifications, so we can deliver alerts about friend activity.

How we use this information

  • To operate the core features of the app: the activity feed, friend connections, stats/leaderboards, and pub groups.
  • To send you notifications about friend requests, reactions, comments, and activity you've opted into.
  • To review reports and enforce blocking between users.

How information is shared

We do not sell your information. Content you post (check-ins, comments, reactions) is visible to other users of the app, consistent with how a social feed works — unless you've blocked that user or they've blocked you, in which case your content is hidden from each other. Your data is stored using Google Firebase infrastructure.

Your choices

  • Block: you can block any user from their profile. Blocking hides their posts and comments from your feed, and ends any friend connection between you.
  • Report: you can report a post or a user directly from the feed or their profile if you see something that concerns you.
  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and everything you've posted at any time from Profile → Danger Zone → Delete My Account. This requires re-entering your password to confirm, and cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

BeerReel involves content about alcohol and is not directed at, or intended for use by, anyone under the legal drinking age. The app requires an age affirmation before it can be used.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
